I'm **Cooper Lappenbusch**. I write here about the things I keep, the habits I'm trying to build, and the small decisions that add up to a considered life.

The premise is simple: most of what crowds our days isn't chosen. It accumulates. Refinement is the slow work of noticing what's there, keeping what matters, and letting the rest go.

## What you'll find

Everything lives under **Posts**: longer pieces on attention, craft, and meaning, alongside short observations collected as they arrive.

## Colophon

This site is built with Svelte 5 and SvelteKit, styled with Tailwind CSS, and set in Helvetica Neue. Every page is written as a markdown file and rendered by a small, hand-made markdown renderer. Publishing is a `git push` — Vercel takes care of the rest.

Less, but better.
