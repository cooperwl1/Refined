Since when did education become so dull?

Dullness is often shown as an excuse for clarity. A dull statement has the clarity of mud.

Educational prose isn't about sounding smart, it's about connecting ideas. Writing to teach is difficult because we have to make constant decisions about how much our audience knows about a given subject. Do they know binomials? Basic algebra? Do they even know addition? Our understanding of the student starts from the base of their knowledge.

My prose attempts to make one statement about the audience; they want to be entertained.
